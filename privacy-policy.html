This Privacy Policy explains how Okota & Associates Advocates ("the Firm", "I", "me") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (No. 24 of 2019) and its subsidiary regulations. By submitting information through this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

01 — Who We Are

Okota & Associates Advocates is a sole proprietorship law firm registered and practising in Kenya, with offices at Wazo Building, 1st Floor, Suna Migori. The Firm is the data controller in respect of personal data submitted through this website.

Contact details: okotalaw@yahoo.com  |  0729 082 553

02 — Data We Collect

When you submit the consultation request form on this website, I collect the following personal data:

  • Identity data: first name and last name
  • Contact data: phone number and email address
  • Matter data: your selected area of law and your written description of your legal matter

I do not collect any data automatically through cookies, analytics tools, or tracking technologies on this website. I do not use advertising networks or profiling tools.

Important note on matter descriptions: Depending on what you write in the message field, your submission may contain sensitive personal data — for example, health information (personal injury), family circumstances (family law), or criminal allegations (criminal defense). This data is treated with the highest level of confidentiality consistent with legal professional privilege and the KDPA.

03 — How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your consultation request and communicate with you regarding your legal matter
  • To assess whether the Firm is able to assist with your matter
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations applicable to advocates practising in Kenya
  • To maintain records as required by the Law Society of Kenya and applicable law

Your data will not be used for marketing, profiling, automated decision-making, or any purpose unrelated to your legal matter without your explicit consent.

04 — Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, I process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Section 30 KDPA): By submitting the consultation form, you consent to the processing of your personal data for the purposes described in this policy.
  • Legitimate interests (Section 30(b) KDPA): Processing is necessary to respond to your inquiry and provide legal services.
  • Legal obligation: Certain records may be required to be maintained under the Advocates Act (Cap. 16) and related regulations.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting me at okotalaw@yahoo.com. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out.

05 — Third-Party Service Providers

I use the following third-party services in the operation of this website. These providers may process your personal data on my behalf:

  • EmailJS (Revenium Inc., USA): Used to transmit consultation form submissions to my email inbox. When you submit the form, your name, phone number, email address, selected practice area, and message are transmitted to EmailJS servers. EmailJS acts as a data processor. Their privacy policy is available at emailjs.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Inc., USA): The "Chat with us" button on this website opens a WhatsApp conversation. If you use this feature, your communication is subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. I do not share your data with WhatsApp unless you initiate a conversation.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA): This website loads fonts from Google's servers. Your IP address may be logged by Google when fonts are loaded. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties without your explicit consent, except where required by law or a court order.

06 — Cross-Border Data Transfers

Some of the third-party providers listed above (EmailJS, WhatsApp, Google) are based in the United States of America, which may not offer the same level of data protection as Kenya. Where your data is transferred outside Kenya, I take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including relying on the contractual terms of those service providers.

By submitting information through this website, you acknowledge and consent to such transfers as described in this policy.

07 — Data Retention

I retain your personal data for the following periods:

  • Consultation enquiries that do not proceed to a client relationship: up to 12 months from the date of submission, after which data is securely deleted.
  • Data relating to an established attorney-client relationship: for a minimum of 7 years from the date the matter is closed, in accordance with the Advocates Act and professional obligations, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Data held by third-party processors (e.g. EmailJS): subject to the respective provider's retention policies.

After the applicable retention period, your personal data will be securely deleted or anonymised.

08 — Your Rights Under the KDPA

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to AccessRequest a copy of the personal data I hold about you (Section 26 KDPA).
Right to RectificationRequest correction of inaccurate or incomplete data held about you (Section 27 KDPA).
Right to ErasureRequest deletion of your data where there is no lawful reason for its continued processing (Section 28 KDPA).
Right to ObjectObject to processing of your personal data in certain circumstances (Section 29 KDPA).
Right to Data PortabilityReceive your personal data in a structured, commonly used format.
Right to Withdraw ConsentWithdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me using the details in Section 12. I will respond within 21 days as required by the KDPA. If you are dissatisfied with my response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya.

09 — Data Security

I implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, or alteration. These measures include:

  • Secure transmission of form data via encrypted channels (HTTPS)
  • Restricted access to client data on a need-to-know basis
  • Use of reputable third-party processors with their own security protocols

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While I strive to protect your personal data, I cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in risk to your rights, I will notify you and the ODPC as required by law.

10 — Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 18. I do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted personal data through this website, please contact me immediately and I will take steps to delete that information.

11 — Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the law or my data practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of this website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Material changes that affect your rights will be communicated prominently on this website.
